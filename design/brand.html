Peekover — Brand Sheet v1

Raw in. Reading out. · Free, for everyone, in plain sight.

The idea in one line

Peekover is the trusted way to read the raw files computers (and AI agents) produce. The brand is a reader’s brand: paper, ink, and one highlighter — nothing else. Interactive things are ink underlined in highlighter; the restraint IS the identity. Playful where it greets you, invisible where you read.

Logo

  • The mark: two eyes peeking over a yellow document. Three sacred shapes; size-specific renditions live in peekover-icons.html. Logo internals always use fixed ink #16171F and fixed paper #FBFBF8 — never theme tokens — so contrast holds in dark mode.
  • The blink (6s cycle) exists only in product UI and the website header — never in store icons or print.
  • Wordmark: “Peekover” set in Bricolage Grotesque 700, letter-spacing −0.01em, one word, capital D and P.
  • Clear space = one eye-radius; minimum size with wordmark = 24px tall.

The yellow rule: highlighter appears only at signature moments — logo, active TOC mark, progress bar, text marks, one accent per screen region. If yellow is everywhere, it is nowhere. Sepia theme swaps paper for #F6EEDF and ink for #3B2F1E. Why not blue/indigo: blurple is the documented “sea of sameness” in tech branding — a chromatic accent kept fighting the yellow (we tried orange, then red) — so there isn’t one. Monochrome + one highlighter is rarer than any accent color, and can never clash.

Voice & tone

Sounds like us

“Raw in. Reading out.” — short, declarative, confident.

“Your files never leave your machine.” — trust stated as fact, not marketing.

“The # deserves better.” — playful, but about the craft.

“Free, for everyone, in plain sight.” — the deal, plainly.

Doesn’t

“Revolutionize your markdown workflow!” — hype verbs, exclamation marks.

“Military-grade privacy.” — vague superlatives; we show receipts instead.

“Unlock premium features.” — there are none; never borrow freemium language.

Jokes at the reader’s expense. The eyes are friendly, never snarky.

Brand behaviors (the part people remember)

  • Trust is shown, not claimed: the status bar reads “no network calls”; the network dot turns amber when the user makes one. Every marketing claim links to code.
  • The document is the hero: chrome recedes, auto-hides, whispers. If a UI element competes with paragraph one, the element loses.
  • Peek = instant: anything slower than “instant” is a bug in the brand, not just the product.
  • Free means free, permanently: no ads, no tiers, no funnels — stated in the footer of everything.
