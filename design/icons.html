Peekover Icon Set

One mark, four renditions. The rule: as the canvas shrinks, detail leaves before character does — text lines go first, then outlines thicken, and at 16 px the mark reduces to its three essential shapes: two eyes, one yellow document. Pupils always look slightly down-right, toward the page being read.

Construction rules

  • Three shapes are sacred: two eyes + one yellow document. Everything else is negotiable per size.
  • Detail exits in order: second text line → first text line → outlines → done. Never remove an eye or the document.
  • Pupils look down-right — toward the page. Centered pupils read as staring at the user; avoid.
  • The blink is UI-only. Icons never animate in stores or toolbars; the blink lives in the product header and website.
  • Yellow is the constant: #FFE94A light · #FFD84D dark. Ink: #16171F / #0B0C12 on dark backgrounds.
  • Clear space = one eye-radius on all sides. Nothing enters it, including the wordmark.
  • Never: gradients, shadows, outlines around the whole mark, rotation, or recoloring the eyes.
  • Export: ship PNGs rendered from these exact SVGs at 1× and 2× (Chrome wants 16/32/48/128 PNG in the manifest).
