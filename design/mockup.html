Welcome to Peekover

Raw text goes in. Reading comes out. This page is a live tour of everything your markdown can look like — everything on it works.

NOTE
Your files never leave your machine. Rendering happens locally, permissions stay minimal, and the source is open — peek at it any time.

Reading, upgraded#

Peekover turns a wall of plain text into a page you actually want to read — typeset, navigable, and instant. Open a file, a folder, or a raw URL; the table of contents follows you, and watch mode keeps the page live while your editor (or your AI agent1Footnote 1. Claude Code, Cursor, and friends write a lot of markdown. Peekover is where you read it — hover previews included.) writes.

It handles the whole GFM family: old drafts get struck through, emoji shortnames like :rocket: become 🚀, and alerts come in every flavor:

WARNING
Other viewers ship 11 MB and phone home. Peekover does neither — see the receipts.

In plain sight#

No servers. No telemetry. No network calls unless you make one — and a visible indicator when you do. The manifest is annotated line-by-line, builds are reproducible, and the whole project is MIT-licensed. That’s the deal: free, for everyone, in plain sight.
